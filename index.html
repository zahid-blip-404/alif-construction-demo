Who we are

Dhaka's public infrastructure, delivered to contractor standards

Alif Construction Limited is a civil construction company established in Dhaka in June 2019, enlisted on e-GP for Government of Bangladesh procurement. We deliver under Italian-Thai Development for DMTCL, and directly for the Public Works Department.

Our vision

To be the subcontractor of record on the works Bangladesh is proudest of, trusted wherever the public is watching.

Our mission

Deliver civil, renovation and architectural works to engineer approval, on live public sites, with one desk accountable.
