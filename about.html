About us

A Dhaka civil construction company trusted with the works Bangladesh watches most closely.

Who we are

Alif Construction Limited is a civil construction company from Dhaka, established in June 2019, delivering construction, renovation and architectural works on the projects the public actually uses: the metro corridor above the street, the railings and footpaths on it, and the government buildings behind the gates.

We work as the subcontractor of record under main contractors and directly for agencies: qualified on MRT Line-6 under Italian-Thai Development for DMTCL, and trusted by the Public Works Department in its own bhaban. Every package runs through one desk, from tender to handover.

Our visionTo be the subcontractor of record on the works Bangladesh is proudest of, trusted wherever the city is watching.
Our missionDeliver civil, renovation and architectural works to engineer approval, on live public sites, safely and on programme.
Safety firstInducted crews, controlled work zones and a named safety lead on every site, beside traffic, stations and offices.
Everything on paperMaterials, methods and revisions enter site through reviewed submissions. The record is ready before it is asked for.
